## Rules / mechanics stated
- Deck size variable: 25 to 40 cards (aggro small deck vs slower 40). Starting health scales with deck size per speaker (40-card deck = more health than 28/30-card deck).
- Commander has its own modules in addition to cards. Speaker plays a Terran commander (name heard as "Admirable ... Borodina", low confidence); uses 2 modules: Drop Pods and Rally. Other modules unowned; currency "scrap" buys cards.
- Two resources: Supply (mana-like; yellow inverted triangle on cards) and Energy (black circle number; separate pool, "hero power"-like; used by some modules and Energize effects). Module Rally costs no energy per speaker. Energy totals grow (opponent seen at 5, 11, 17, 18 energy; speaker 3).
- Card types/tags mentioned: tactic, support, neutral, unit; races: aberration, cyborgs, massives, mercenaries.
- Keywords as explained (speaker partly unsure): Firepower = increases attack of tactic cards (not support). Soak N = damage reduced by N when hit. Armored = takes 1 less damage (later says takes only 1 damage no matter what; low confidence). Screen = taunt-like. Mobility = can attack the turn played. Energize N = extra effect, must be chosen/paid with energy (e.g. Energize 8 gains screen); speaker admits not understanding it fully. Activate = ability of unit (e.g. draw a card, copy text). Hidden effect cards not revealed until enemy triggers.
- Passing turn via side button; attacking hero/commander directly; board-wipe effects exist; sniper-like card can return a unit to hand for energy.
- Ranked game gives credits/new cards and levels; mulligan at game start (trade cards).
