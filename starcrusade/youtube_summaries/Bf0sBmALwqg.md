## Rules / mechanics facts (as stated)
- Knowing the opponent's hand size is very important, especially when playing Consortium (консорциум) on "dislocation" (дислокация; meaning unclear, possibly the player's side/seat or game mode, low confidence).
- The game has no feature (yet) to hover the mouse over the opponent's hand to show the card count; players must count manually.
- Many experienced players miscount, so they misjudge the "cost of a hand card" (Кост врука, as transcribed, garbled) of Betrayal-type cards and "Error" cards.
- Example: with 10 cards in my hand, if the opponent plays Betrayal, they effectively throw away a valuable card for 2 resources, gain 1 credit, and deal me no damage.
- Playing the "Error" card effectively saves my card, because the card that "burns" (is discarded/wasted) is the Error card, not mine.
- Counting method: the hand is laid out symmetrically about screen center (center is above the "second module", unclear). The opponent's right side tilts/fans more than the left, so it is hard to count reliably. Count the left side, then double it.
  - Even hand: the two halves meet at a joint; count one half and multiply by 2 (example: 5 on the left means 10 total, though visually only 3-4 seemed visible on the right).
  - Odd hand: a single straight card sits in the middle; count one side, double it, add 1 (example: 4 per side + 1 = 9).
  - Third example: 4 per side = 8.
- Screenshot examples: apparent counts 8/9, 8, 7 were actually 10, 9, 8.
