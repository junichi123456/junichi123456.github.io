# GlLJu-poXSM - Star Crusade sponsored first-look (Christopher Rod)
Language: en (auto captions; names mangled). Early-access Steam, F2P, sponsored.

## Rules / mechanics (as stated)
- Factions named: Consortium, Terran ("tan/Teran"), "hair Gog" (unclear, likely Haj), Anunnaki, Shanti, Hierarchy. Factions unlocked by beating that faction.
- Terran focus listed: board clearing, mass assault, bulky ships, big artillery, traditional battlefield tactics, command structure, battlefield placement. Shanti: genetic manipulation, aberration, card modification (mutate, transform).
- Deck 25 to ~40 cards ("I think 40"); commander health = number of cards in deck (25 cards = 25 HP). Speaker thinks ~30 is a sweet spot.
- Up to 3 modules (unlocked; commander abilities, cost supplies).
- Supplies = resource to play cards. Energy accrues from actions; some cards spend it. Initiative: gain an extra supply.
- Credit (Consortium): play for lower cost but lose supply crates (triangles) next turn; "borrowing".
- Keywords: soak N (absorbs N damage), screen (must be attacked first), cloak (untargetable until it attacks), cipher (opponent can't see card played), revenge (triggers on unit destroyed), shield (removed after absorbing initial damage), armored (takes only 1 damage per hit), pacify (can't attack, can still block), energize (extra effect by spending energy), mutate/mutagen, regeneration, nullify. Mercenary is a card type tag.
- Placement/adjacency matters. Rarities: regular, conscript, elite, heroic, paragon (one rare guaranteed per pack). Raid = draft-style mode. Packs 5 cards (~$9 CAD); 5 credits per win; challenges ~30 credits. Matchmaking guessed from rank + hero XP.
- Shanti commander: mutagens (lose 1 HP, gain 2 attack for turn).
