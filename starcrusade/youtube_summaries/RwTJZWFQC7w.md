# RwTJZWFQC7w (Tiny Grimes Games, Consortium ramp deck) | language: en

## Rules/mechanics
- Franchise: gives an "empty supply crate" for the whole game; play it turn 1-3, permanently +1 supply (ramp). Deck runs 2 copies; double Franchise considered undesirable. Considered cutting to 25-card deck to see it more.
- Consortium ramp plan: turn-3 Rapid Strike Team (normally turn 4), 4-drop on turn 3-4 etc.; ramps to 7-8 drops (few 8-drops exist).
- Modules: deck has Soak (given to units, lets them survive/take hits), and Trader in the Ranks (decoy; other modules "don't take energy").
- Reaching rank 15 unlocks a second free module. Season reset sends player to rank 30. Beta turn timer exists; opponent voice-line jokes.
- Promo code "tiny Grimes" gives 3 packs. Mulligan used to find Franchise. Opponent Firepower hero power fetches a tactic from deck; assault, psychic charge (Nukan opp) mentioned.
