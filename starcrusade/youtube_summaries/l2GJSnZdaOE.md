# l2GJSnZdaOE - "Star Crusade CCG: гайд на Гиперфэйсгога" (Hyper-aggression / face deck)
- Language: Russian (auto-captions, heavily garbled; many mappings uncertain). Narrator is #78 in a deck-guide series.
- Deck: "Гиперагрессия" (Hyper-aggression), Hajir-Gog-flavoured (Hellfire Cannon, Frenzy, Adrenal Overload, Warlord's Call) with Syndicate/Hierarchy splash cards. Faction not explicitly stated.
- Pitched as arguably the best deck to start with and the cheapest: no paragons, no heroes, costs only 185 raw materials ("сырьё").
- Module list: only the second module is stated, unlocked after level 15 (Warlord's Call). Deck size not stated.

## Strategy as stated
- Go face only. The most you spend on enemy units is the "ping" damage; everything else hits the commander. Do not control the enemy board.
- Mulligan for: gun (Hellfire Cannon), Syndicate Enforcers, Corrupted Hound; likes Meteor Rain in the starting hand.
- Fast, short games, so good for farming gold. Claimed rank: around 5 is very realistic, Emperor unlikely.
- Weaknesses: inconsistent (if the gun doesn't arrive in time you probably lose), Syndicate Enforcers stuck at the bottom of the deck hurts, and very sensitive to walls (any damage into walls hurts).
