## Rules / mechanics stated
- Hearthstone-like sci-fi CCG; 6 factions (more planned), each aggressive/defensive/brute force/calculated.
- Starting health = number of cards in deck. Decks 40 cards (his Hajir deck built to 40; deck levels up, lvl 3 -> 5, Terran deck lvl 7).
- Each round you earn more supply points (resource) used to play cards; round N gives N supply (2 on round 2, 3 on round 3, 5 on 5...). Some cards give +1 supply until end of turn.
- Opening: keep or replace cards; discard expensive ones (a 7-supply card mentioned).
- Commander has a unique special ability. Hajir/first deck commander: deals one damage (direct attack). Terran deck commander: deploys a Guardsman.
- Attacking uses no supply. Newly played units cannot attack that turn. Units cannot target commander with unit-targeting damage cards.
- Stats: attack / defense(health); skull icon = both die.
- Modules/perks: a couple of customizable slots, activate each round; one gave extra energy at end of every turn. Energy is spent for unit abilities (sniper: spend 4 energy, deal 1 damage, return unit to hand - med).
- Screen = taunt/shield: enemies must attack this unit before the commander. Pacifist screen cannot attack.
- Keywords seen: Screen, Critical, Assault, Deploy (effect only when played from deploy), Special attack(s).
- Rewards: XP, deck levels unlock new cards. Modes: bot practice, online, ranked. Planned: trading, 2v2, PVE leagues, cash tournaments. Daily challenges: deal 15 damage with tactic cards, win 2 games with Terran, win 2 vs a friend.
